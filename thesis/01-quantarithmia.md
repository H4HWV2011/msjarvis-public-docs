## 1.5 Power Geometries: Maximopolies and Megaopolies

Quantarithmia distinguishes two geometries of power:

- **Maximopolies:** Actors and structures that control capital flows — landholding
  structures, financing chains, large asset owners.
- **Megaopolies:** Actors and structures that control operations or platforms —
  logistics networks, payment processors, cloud services, data platforms.

In the July 2026 stack these are not just words. They appear as:

- GBIM entities and beliefs about land, corporations, and infrastructures

  local per-user experience and aggregated community signals

For rural developers, this means a maximopoly might be visible in GBIM as "who

working, governed way to represent some of it.

1. Queries `public.public_admissible_gbim_mv` — the ten-condition public evidence
